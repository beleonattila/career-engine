# Writing style

**Short version** (read first): plain, specific, first person. Facts with numbers over adjectives.
No buzzwords, no claims you cannot back with background.md or competencies.md.

Apply these rules to every piece of text /cv generates: profile paragraph, cover letter, emails.

- **Register:** (formal / warm / direct — your choice)
