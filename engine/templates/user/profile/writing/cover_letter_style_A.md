# Cover letter — Style A

*The default letter /cv writes (Step 4). Style B (cover_letter_style_B.md) is the alternative.*

## Voice directives

- Follow writing_style.md. Open with the role and why you, not with a greeting formula.
- Every claim traces to background.md or competencies.md.

## Structure

1. **Opening** — the role, and the one reason you fit (2–3 sentences).
2. **Technical match** — the two or three requirements you meet, each with evidence.
3. **What makes this application different** — a project, result or angle no other applicant has.
4. **Close** — what you want next (a conversation), one or two sentences.
