# Cover letter — Style B (organisation-centred)

*Starts from the organisation's goal instead of your record. /cv Step 4b.*

## What makes B different from A

- A leads with your fit; B leads with their goal and how you would serve it.

## Inherited rules (non-negotiable)

- All of writing_style.md and the factual-grounding rule.

## Structure

1. **Motivation** — why this organisation's work, specifically (3–5 short sentences).
2. **Their goal, your contribution** (3–4 sentences).
3. **Professional match** — what you can do (4–5 sentences, or 2–3 plus 3 bullets).
4. **Personal match** — how you work (2–3 sentences).
5. **Close** (1–2 sentences).
